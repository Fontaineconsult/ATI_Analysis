## Outcome 1 · the four process elements

### A. Classify barriers against the essential functions of a library resource
- **What are the essential functions of a library resource, in your words?** Push for a list.
  Candidate: search, retrieve full text, read the text, export a citation. Their list wins.
- Which of those does a keyboard-navigation failure break? (This is the live blocker on the
  pending requests — confirm it is understood the same way on both sides.)
- What would count as *not* severe — a barrier you would want approved without a plan?

### B. Maintain a standing alternative for each barrier class
Walk **each class** separately. Per class: the route, who performs it, turnaround, annual volume.
- **Text content** (untagged PDF, no OCR) — scanning/remediation on request. Who? How long?
- **Time-based media** (uncaptioned video/audio) — is there any route at all today? *(Likely
  the weakest class — probe hard.)*
- **Interactive components** (search widgets, viewers, embedded tools) — is there any route,
  or is this where referral is the only answer?
- **Static non-text** (images, charts, maps) — described how, by whom?
- For each: **can it be reached without asking a person?** (Independence is one of the six
  equally-effective conditions and request-gating fails it — record the honest answer.)

### C. Post the statement and contact at the point of access
- Which pages are the actual points of access? (database A–Z, research guides, catalog,
  reference desk.) Who has edit rights to each?
- Who is the named contact users are directed to?
- **Ticketing:** what category/route should a library access problem take? Who triages it?
  Does it reach the alternate-format person directly?

### D. Obtain conformance documentation at selection *(not in Dan's email — add it)*
- Who talks to vendors, and at what point in the selection cycle?
- Could a VPAT/ACR be requested **before** the requisition is raised?
  *(This is the cheapest change on the table: "no VPAT available" is an independent TAAP
  trigger in the SFBRN procedure, and asking earlier removes it outright.)*
- Renewals too, or only new purchases?
