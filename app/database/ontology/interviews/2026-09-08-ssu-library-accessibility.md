### 7.11-ins — current Defined → Established needs 4 of 4
"Developed a process that incorporates accessibility when acquiring, converting,
digitizing, creating, and maintaining library assets."
Bar source: companion guide.

The strongest prep material in the graph: five notes describe the practice
fluently. Requested collection items are scanned and digitized as accessible PDFs.
Document delivery falls back to InterLibrary Loan. Database vendors are required to
submit a VPAT or accessibility statement at acquisition. Print materials route
through assistive technology in the Library and DSS, with Alternate Media requests
for conversions. That is fluency, and fluency evidences Procedures at best: nothing
in the record shows a stored workflow document, a named owner, or output records.
The only wired evidence is the SSU Alternative Services node, unowned.
