## Protocol reminder

Identity round → walkthrough → bar sweep → commitments versus wishes → pending
decisions → artifacts → recap aloud.

Say the level out loud and negotiate it in the room. Getting agreement on the honest
level converts the gap into their action item instead of ours. Voice-tag action
items, open questions, and decisions for the recording, and spell proper nouns once.
