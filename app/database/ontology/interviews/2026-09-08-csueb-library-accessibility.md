### 7.11-ins — current Defined → Established needs 4 of 4
"Developed a process that incorporates accessibility when acquiring, converting,
digitizing, creating, and maintaining library assets."
Bar source: companion guide.

The current note is adverse and specific: "No formal process, but Procurement
requires VPAT reviews prior to acquiring/purchasing. Accessible Media services in
Accessibility Services and the Learning Commons in the Library assist with
conversions." Adverse testimony caps the grade until a formal process exists. The
only evidence on file is the Chancellor's Office Access to eResources service,
which is systemwide, not a campus practice.

| Element | On file today | Ask | Would evidence it |

| Procedures | "No formal process" (note); VPAT review sits in Procurement; Learning Commons assists conversions | Walk me through the last database purchase and the last digitization request, start to finish. Who did each step? | A written workflow covering acquisition, digitization, and maintenance |
| Output | NONE | What record does a conversion or an acquisition review leave? Could you export a year of them? | Conversion logs, VPAT review records for library purchases |

Verdict to reach live: a named owner plus a stored workflow document moves this to

process and its records. Do not claim Established on Procedures: assistance on
