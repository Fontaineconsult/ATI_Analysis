The Return of JEDI PIE certificate series focuses on culturally responsive
and historically aware teaching through two asynchronous courses—A
New Hope (required first) and Do or Do Not—which together provide
practical JEDI teaching strategies and can each be completed to earn a
badge. Participants must complete these courses to participate in JEDI
teaching square.

Mental Health First Aid
Certificate Trainings
Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) is an early intervention training that
equips faculty and staff to recognize and respond to students
experiencing mental health challenges. Training offered three times a
year to support student retention and well-being. Participants must
attend the entire training to receive a certificate.
