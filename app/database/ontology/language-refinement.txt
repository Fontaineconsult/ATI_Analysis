Dawna, can accessibility become a fourth Faculty Learning Circle focus area?
Zach's read on the call was that attendance is the core problem. The content is good, but it only reaches people who turn up. We could not find a route to peer-to-peer advocacy in the room. The faculty who do this work well tend to be adjunct, and the work carries no compensation. Do the Learning Circles already solve that? They pay 800 dollars a semester to a lead facilitator and 500 to each participant, across Belonging, Sustaining Ourselves, and Intentional Teaching Using AI. None of the three is accessibility. This needs a fourth theme rather than new money, which is why it is the item on this list I would push hardest for.

Dawna or Cheryl, did the Title II workshop series run again this year?

Is there a reason nobody mentioned it on the call? The record currently treats that series as something that ran in 2024-25 and finished, because that is how the page reads.
