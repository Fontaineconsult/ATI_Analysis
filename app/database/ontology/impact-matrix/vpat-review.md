# SFBRN VPAT review procedure

This procedure governs ACR interpretation and the derivation of the findings, Severity, Breadth
and Veracity that the rubric in `rubric-core.md` maps to a final decision. Both documents share
one set of terms and constants, with the rubric governing any conflict.

Each section maps to a numbered rubric section, and each *To be written* section lists its
planned content.

## 1. Terms

Maps to the rubric section Terms.

*To be written.*

- The rubric terms this document relies on: review standard, core task, functional group,
  nonconformance, barrier, finding, ACR lead.
- VPAT and ACR, and the VPAT editions in use (WCAG, 508, EU, INT).
- The ACR conformance terms: Supports, Partially Supports, Does Not Support, Not Applicable,
  Not Evaluated.
- Remark, and what a usable remark contains.

## 2. Intake inputs

Maps to rubric section 1, Inputs and symbols.

*To be written.*

- The core task list: how the reviewer obtains the requester's minimum functional
  requirements from Procurement Step 1 and confirms the list.
- The interfaces and platforms being bought, so the ACR can be matched to them.
- The product version, so the ACR can be matched to it.

## 3. Exposure and review depth

Maps to rubric section 2, Exposure, and section 5, Veracity.

*To be written.*

- How Exposure and Severity set the Veracity floor, and so how deep the ACR review goes.
- Which exposure answers the reviewer verifies during ACR review.

## 4. ACR quality checks

Maps to rubric section 5, Veracity (E1-, E1 and E1+).

*To be written.*

- The quality checks in `ACR_CHECKS`, with how to apply each one.
- The checks the ACR research adds: remarks that contradict their rating, hedge words, scope
  carve-outs, and single-browser or single-screen-reader testing.
- The GSA issue-level supplement, and when it lifts an ACR to E1+.

## 5. From ACR rows to findings

Maps to the rubric section Terms (ACR lead, barrier and finding).

*To be written.*

- Which rows are ACR leads.
- Mapping a success criterion to the functional groups it affects.
- Placing a lead's feature on a core task, or off every core task.
- One nonconformance producing several findings.
- Leads whose remark names no feature.

## 6. Severity from a finding

Maps to rubric section 3, Severity.

*To be written.*

- Anchors and worked examples for S1, S2 and S3, per functional group.
- Why the conformance label and the criterion level never set severity.
- Barriers off every core task.
- Recording S2 findings so stacking and spread can be counted.

## 7. Breadth from findings

Maps to rubric section 4, Breadth.

*To be written.*

- Recording task and functional group pairs consistently, so blocked pairs and the S2 count
  come out the same for any reviewer.

## 8. Evidence beyond the ACR

Maps to rubric section 5, Veracity (E2a, E2b and E3).

*To be written.*

- A quick spot check of the ACR, and when a contradiction drops it to E1-.
- The vendor demonstration script for core tasks with assistive technology.
- Third-party audits, and what a named method looks like.
- ATI team testing of core tasks.

## 9. Routes from ACR review

Maps to rubric section 6, Routes.

*To be written.*

- No ACR at all (route 1).
- Evidence below the Veracity floor (route 3), how to raise it to the next level, and when to
  record that it cannot be raised.
- No TAAP on file (route 4), with the handoff in section 10.

## 10. Handoff to the TAAP and the roadmap

Maps to rubric sections 8 and 9, Remedy and Requirements by final decision.

*To be written.*

- Writing findings into the TAAP's Known Accessibility Barriers section.
- Writing findings into the vendor's Accessibility Roadmap.
- The TAAP accessibility statement content that comes from the findings.

## 11. Renewal and re-review

Maps to rubric section 9 and the ACR maximum age constant in section 10.

*To be written.*

- ACR age and version at renewal.
- Checking roadmap progress against new findings.
