Exposure

Floor facts

Step 2

Evidence and barriers

Definition

Raising the evidence

The evidence is below the Veracity floor. The ATI review team records whether a demonstration or its own testing can still raise it.

The fallback applies. The review treats the product as S3 (provisional) with the barriers found as a minimum.

Findings

One row per barrier, by role and disability group on one essential function. An essential function is a function the product exists to provide, such as registering for a class or submitting homework, and it counts as essential when its removal would change the product's use or purpose. No rows means no known barriers. What a finding records.

No findings recorded.
