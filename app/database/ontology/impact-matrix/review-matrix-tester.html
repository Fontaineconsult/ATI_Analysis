Step 2

Evidence and barriers

Definition

The ACR

The analyst reads the date, the author and the remarks off the ACR. Remarks that read like a bug report show the vendor did the evaluation, so the analyst takes the ACR at face value (E1+) unless it is both more than a year old and by the vendor's own staff. Remarks that name the feature but not where or how leave the ACR at E1, and the analyst's spot check comes next, under Raising the evidence. Remarks that are blank, restate the criterion, repeat across rows or hedge lower the ACR and open the remarks checks. The document checks apply to every ACR, and a failed check ranks the ACR E1- until the vendor corrects the report or the analyst accepts the defect. How the review derives the level. How to read an ACR.

Author
A firm the vendor does not own, is not affiliated with, and does not pay for remediation.
Remarks

Read the Remarks and Explanations column on the "Partially Supports" and "Does Not Support" rows. Pick the description that fits most of them.

Each row names the page, screen, component or workflow affected, how it falls short and who is affected, the fields of the GSA issue-level supplement. "Supports" rows on complex criteria give an example or the method. The analyst takes the ACR at face value.
Each row says which function or feature has issues, the floor the VPAT instructions set, and stops there. The analyst does not take the ACR at face value and runs the spot check next.
The same sentence on every row, the criterion's own words, or "some", "most" and "generally". The remark alone cannot place a barrier on an essential function. Opens the remarks checks below.
Report checks

The document checks rate the report's front matter and scope, and they apply to every ACR. Tick each check the report fails. The dates above decide the stale check.

The remarks checks rate the Remarks column. They open when the remarks fall short of a bug report, and a tick applies only while they are open.

Accepting the report defect

The vendor cures a report defect with a corrected report. The analyst may instead accept the ACR as it is when the defect does not bear on this purchase, such as a version line one patch release behind. The analyst cannot accept a stale report.

The ACR is then rated like a clean one, and the determination prints the defect and the acceptance.

Raising the evidence

What raises it.

ATI spot check
A contradicted ACR takes a full manual review or a demonstration, recorded below. A second spot check cannot restore it.
Record the barriers the spot check found as findings below.

The fallback applies. The review treats the product as S3 (provisional) with the barriers found as a minimum.

Recording a demonstration, audit or review replaces the ACR as the strongest evidence on file. The ACR's details and the findings it gave stay on the record.

WCAG failures

Add a row for each WCAG failure the evidence shows. A failure is a "Partially Supports" or "Does Not Support" row in the ACR, or a barrier the analyst found in a demonstration, an audit or ATI testing. Each row is one finding.

Name the essential function the failure affects, the role and disability group it stops, and its severity.

An essential function is one the product exists to provide, such as registering for a class or submitting homework. A function counts as essential when removing it would change the product's use or purpose.

An ACR the analyst takes at face value, with only minor admitted failures, needs no row. What a finding records.
